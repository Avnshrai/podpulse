PodPulse cluster-tunnel agent installed.

The agent is dialing home to:
  {{ .Values.saas.url }}

Watch it come online (typically takes 5-10 seconds):
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs -l app.kubernetes.io/name={{ include "pp-connect.name" . }} -f

Once you see "Connected to proxy" in the logs, the cluster will appear
as Online ✓ on your PodPulse dashboard. From then on every kubectl call
through PodPulse rides this tunnel — no inbound firewall, no VPN.

Uninstall (the cluster row stays in PodPulse, marked Offline):
  helm uninstall {{ .Release.Name }} -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}
